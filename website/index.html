Twelve tools

The four you'll use most, and eight more for the rest.

  1. Convert(28)Audio, video and images into any other format. Batches and hardware encoding.
  2. Dictate(4)Hold a shortcut, speak, and your words are typed into any app.
  3. Compress(4)Videos, audio, photos and PDFs a fraction of the size, or under a limit.
  4. QR(5)Codes for links, Wi-Fi and contacts, or read one from a picture.
  5. CountWords, reading time and a private grammar check.
  6. PDFMerge, split, organise, sign and protect.
  7. CutoutRemove the background from a photo.
  8. ReadPull the text out of pictures and scans.
  9. ResizeExact sizes, with presets for social and web.
  10. RenameHundreds of files at once, with undo.
  11. PalettePick any colour on screen, or from a picture.
  12. CompareSee exactly what changed between two texts.

Opening it the first time

Windows: if it says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Mac: open Morf once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Both appear because Morf isn't signed by a paid certificate yet. After that, it updates itself.
